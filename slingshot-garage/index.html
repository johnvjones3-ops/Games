How to play
  • Launch: drag back from anywhere, aim, release. Further back = more power.
  • Drive: hold ▶ (→ / D) for gas, ◀ (← / A) to brake or reverse.
  • In the air the same buttons tilt the car: gas leans back, brake leans forward.
  • Rocket: hold the rocket button (Space). Refill with blue canisters.
  • Flips, perfect landings and long hang time pay bonus coins.
  • Red cans refuel you. Smash crates for coins. Green pads give a speed boost.
  • Past 2 km you can Evolve in the DNA Lab: start a new world with permanent DNA perks.
  • Gamepads work too: triggers to drive, A for rocket.
